SCENARIOS

The four flames

1 · Closed. Gas mixes with air only at the point of combustion. Incomplete combustion → soot particles heated to incandescence give the luminous yellow “safety flame”. Cool, dirty, easy to see.

2 · Slightly open. A small premixed cone appears at the base, white-hot; the plume above is still sooty orange fading to red at the tip.

3 · Half open. Soot largely gone. A pale blue inner cone with a faint violet mantle.

4 · Fully open. Near-stoichiometric premix: a sharp teal inner cone, an almost invisible blue mantle, and a distinct roar. Hottest, cleanest — and easy to lose against a bright background.
